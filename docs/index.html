How this forecast works

Exponential smoothing updates its estimate using recent sales and a trend. The validation scores compare predictions with the last eight historical weeks. The baseline repeats the last training week for every forecast week. Lower MAE and RMSE mean smaller errors. Returns and cancelled transactions are excluded from the demand series.
